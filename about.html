About the Bethel Location Investigation

This application investigates indoor radon gas behaviors at the Bethel Location. The data was exported directly from certified monitoring hardware after resetting device batteries and relocating the sensor from an above-ground office environment into an unfinished, dig-in basement with limited natural airflow.

Key Environmental & Health Hazards (Beyond Radon)

  • Indoor Particulate Matter (Dust & Silica): Unfinished dig-in basements often accumulate fine mineral dust and crystalline silica that become airborne during humidity shifts, exacerbating respiratory inflammation.
  • Sub-slab Moisture & Mold Volatile Organic Compounds (MVOCs): Hydrostatic pressure pushes invisible moisture vapor through porous foundation walls, fostering hidden fungal colonies whose metabolic gases trigger neurological and immunological stress.
  • Carbon Monoxide & Backdrafting: Negative barometric pressure events caused by severe thunderstorms can pull combustion exhaust (from water heaters or furnaces) backward into sealed living spaces.

Statistical Context & Mitigation Metrics

  • Global & National Prevalence: Approximately 1 in 15 homes in the United States maintain radon levels above the EPA action threshold of 4.0 pCi/L.
  • Regional Exposure: Midwest residential structures show elevated winter/spring baseline radon spikes due to frozen topsoil trapping subterranean gas channels.
